Features

Everything a response team needs

From the first report to the last relief delivery, in one place.

Real-time map

New incidents, teams and shelters appear instantly through WebSocket push. No refreshing.

AI photo triage

Attach a photo and Amazon Rekognition tags what it sees, so responders understand the scene fast.

Smart dispatch

One click assigns the nearest team of the right specialty and calculates an ETA.

Relief supply tracking

Shelter occupancy plus food, water and medical stock, with low-supply warnings and allocation.

Instant alerts

Subscribers get emailed through Amazon SNS, filtered by the severity they care about.

Analytics and hotspots

Response times, severity mix, trends and a heatmap show where help is needed most.

How it works

From report to resolved in three steps

1

Report

A citizen signs up, drops a pin on the map and uploads a photo. The incident is stored and an alert goes out.

2

Dispatch

A responder sees it live, dispatches the nearest suitable team and tracks the ETA on the map.

3

Resolve

Teams mark the incident resolved, supplies are replenished and analytics update automatically.
